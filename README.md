# Features
PixelQR provides a simple and customizable QR code generation experience with multiple input types and detailed design controls.

Generate QR codes for:

URL — Convert websites and links into QR codes.
Text — Turn any text or message into a QR code.
Email — Create QR codes for email addresses.
Phone — Generate QR codes for phone numbers.
Wi-Fi — Create QR codes for quick Wi-Fi sharing.

𔓘 Custom QR Colors
Choose custom colors for your QR code to match your style, brand, or design.

𔓘 QR Pattern
Select different QR pattern styles to give your QR code a unique visual appearance.

𔓘 Corner Frame
Customize the Corner Frame with different available styles.

𔓘 Corner Center
Choose different Corner Center styles to personalize the QR code further.

𔓘 Custom Logo
Upload your own logo and place it at the center of the QR code for a personalized or branded look.

𔓘 Download as Image
Download your final customized QR code as an image file and use it for digital or printed purposes.

# Why Mera wala PixelQR?
PixelQR combines simplicity, customization, and modern design in one QR generation experience.

Instead of creating a basic QR code, users can personalize its color, pattern, corners, and logo to create a QR code that better matches their style or brand.
